# Gallifrey

Gallifrey is a Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric mod that brings the world of Doctor Who to Minecraft.

Explore Gallifrey, home of the Time Lords, travel with a Vortex Manipulator inspired by the one seen in the show, and visit Mars, Skaro, Mondas, the Moon, a prehistoric past, a classic version of Minecraft and a parallel Earth called Pete's World.

📖 **Full guide, recipes and pictures: [the Gallifrey wiki](https://github.com/Cerulix-Team/Gallifrey/wiki)**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🪐 Dimensions
Every portal is lit with a **White Point Star**, and portals remember where you came from, so stepping back through takes you home.

| World | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Gallifrey** | Home of the Time Lords. Ten biomes, including Ash, Maple Jungle, Moonpine, Treeborg and Ulanda forests and the Wasteland. White Point and Sonic Crystal ores. |
| **Mars** and **the Moon** | Airless, low-gravity worlds (about 38% and 16.5% of normal). You'll need a space suit. |
| **Skaro** | Home of the Daleks, with Dalekanium Ore. |
| **Mondas** | Home of the Cybermen: Mondasian Cybermen, Proto-Cybermen and Cybermats. |
| **Prehistoric** | An ancient world with prehistoric trees, Prehistoric Ore and cavemen. |
| **Classic** and **Classic Nether** | Minecraft as it used to be. |
| **Pete's World** | A parallel Earth with its own seed. |
| **The Lost Reality** | You'll find out... |

### ⌚ Vortex Manipulator
"Cheap and nasty time travel." Jump to coordinates, saved locations, other players or any dimension (including other mods' dimensions). It has 10 jumps per charge, refuelled with an Atrium Core. It also has an isomorphic lock, a block-incoming setting and a self-destruct. Every jump between dimensions has a small chance of drifting somewhere you didn't plan to go.

### 🔧 Sonic Screwdriver and Sonic Shades
- **Interaction, Overload and Scanning modes:** open doors, flip levers, light candles, power lamps, prime TNT, shatter glass, and scan blocks and mobs.
- **Sonic Workshop:** recharges your sonic and swaps between **16 casings**.
- **Sonic Shades:** a hands-free sonic with a live on-screen scanner.

### ⚙️ Materials and crafting
- **Ores and metals:** Atrium, Sonic Crystal, White Point, Dalekanium, Mars Crystal, Prehistoric Ore, steel and Metalertanium.
- **Circuits:** Blank, Location, Interface and Dimension circuits, which power the mod's gadgets.
- **Tree Tapper:** a Stardew Valley-style tapper. Tap living trees for Silicone, Treeborg Paste, Maple Syrup and Huon Particles.
- **Wood sets:** Ash, Classic, Maple, Moonpine, Prehistoric, TARDIS, Treeborg, Ulanda and Wasted, each with the full set of blocks.

### 🛡️ Gear
- **Tools and armour** in Gallifrey's metals, Mars Stone and Skaro Stone.
- **A space suit** with its own oxygen supply for the airless worlds.
- **Clothing**, including Timelord Robes and hats.
- **Two accessory slots:** Sonic Shades and fez. They show on your character and appear in the survival and creative inventories.

### 👥 Mobs and villagers
- **TimeLords**, **Mondasian Cybermen**, **Proto-Cybermen**, **Cybermats** and **cavemen**.
- **Classic villagers**, **alpha ghasts** and **alpha zombie pigmen** in the Classic worlds.
- **The Time Lord Trader:** a villager profession that trades Gallifrey items, sometimes for classic emeralds.

### 🖥️ Title screen
- A custom Gallifrey logo, with special versions on certain dates.
- **Seven animated backgrounds** to pick from with the button in the bottom-left corner (or in Mod Menu): Miracle (the time vortex), Time Winds, the Time War, the Void, the Untempered Schism, the Eye of Harmony and Trustable. Or keep the normal panorama.
- **Add your own backgrounds** with a resource pack or an addon mod: see [the guide below](#-adding-your-own-title-screen-backgrounds).

### 🎮 And more
- **Game rules:** including starting the game on Gallifrey or in Pete's World.
- **Advancements** for exploring every world.
- **A tidy creative tab**, split into sections.

## 📦 Requirements
- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1
- **Mod loader:** Fabric Loader 0.14.22 or newer
- **Java:** 17 or newer
- **Required mods:** [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and [GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib) 4.8.3 or newer

Custom Portal API, Terraform Wood API and Biolith come bundled inside the Gallifrey jar, so you don't need to install them.

## 🛠️ Installation
1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.20.1.
2. Put **Fabric API**, **GeckoLib** and **Gallifrey** in your `mods` folder.
3. Launch Minecraft with your Fabric profile.

## 🌀 Adding your own title screen backgrounds

Anyone can add new animated backgrounds to the title screen, with a **resource pack** or an **addon mod**. They appear after Gallifrey's own when you click the background button (bottom-left of the title screen) or cycle through them in Mod Menu.

A background is a **folder of frames** and a small **JSON file** that names it.

### What you need
1. **Frames:** your animation as numbered images: `00.jpg`, `01.jpg`, `02.jpg` … (after `99` they carry on as `100`, `101` …).
   - **Square images work best.** The animation is scaled to cover the whole screen, centred. Gallifrey's own use 720×720, 30 frames a second, 90 frames (a 3-second loop).
   - **JPG or PNG.** JPG keeps file sizes small.
   - **Make it loop:** the last frame should lead smoothly back into the first.
2. **A JSON file** at `assets/<namespace>/gallifrey_backgrounds/<name>.json`.
   - `<namespace>` is your pack's or mod's own ID.
   - `<name>` is the background's ID.
   - Both may only use lowercase letters, numbers and `_`.

### The JSON file
Every field is optional:

```json
{
  "name": "Menu: My Vortex",
  "frames": "mypack:menu_vortex/my_vortex",
  "frame_count": 90,
  "fps": 30,
  "format": "jpg"
}
```

| Field | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | What the background button says. Plain text, or a translation key from your lang file. | `gui.<namespace>.menu_background.<name>` |
| `frames` | The folder the frames are in. | `<namespace>:menu_vortex/<name>` |
| `frame_count` | How many frames there are (1–1000). | `90` |
| `fps` | Frames per second (up to 120). | `30` |
| `format` | `"jpg"` or `"png"`. | `"jpg"` |

If something's wrong with the file, the background is skipped and `latest.log` says why. Search it for `[Gallifrey]`.

### With a resource pack
Here's a pack that adds a background called "My Vortex":

```
My Vortex Pack/
├── pack.mcmeta
└── assets/
    └── mypack/
        ├── gallifrey_backgrounds/
        │   └── my_vortex.json
        └── menu_vortex/
            └── my_vortex/
                ├── 00.jpg
                ├── 01.jpg
                ├── …
                └── 89.jpg
```

`pack.mcmeta` (format 15 is Minecraft 1.20.1):

```json
{
  "pack": {
    "pack_format": 15,
    "description": "Adds the My Vortex title screen background"
  }
}
```

`my_vortex.json` only needs a name, because the frames are already in the default folder:

```json
{
  "name": "Menu: My Vortex"
}
```

Put the pack in your `resourcepacks` folder and turn it on. "Menu: My Vortex" then appears when you cycle the background button. Turn the pack off and it disappears again; you don't need to restart.

### With an addon mod
It's exactly the same, inside your mod's resources: `src/main/resources/assets/<your mod id>/…`. Fabric loads every mod's assets automatically, so no code is needed, and your mod doesn't even need to depend on Gallifrey.

```
src/main/resources/assets/mymod/
├── gallifrey_backgrounds/
│   └── nebula.json
├── menu_vortex/
│   └── nebula/
│       ├── 00.png
│       └── …
└── lang/
    └── en_us.json
```

`nebula.json`, using a translation key and PNG frames:

```json
{
  "name": "gui.mymod.menu_background.nebula",
  "format": "png",
  "frame_count": 60
}
```

And in `en_us.json`:

```json
{
  "gui.mymod.menu_background.nebula": "Menu: Nebula"
}
```

### Replacing Gallifrey's own backgrounds
A resource pack can also swap out the built-in animations. Put frames with the same names at the same paths:
- **Time Winds, Time War, the Void, the Untempered Schism and the Eye of Harmony:** the frames are in `assets/gallifrey/menu_vortex/<time_winds | time_war | the_void | untempered_schism | eye_of_harmony>/`, numbered 00–89, as JPG.
- **Trustable:** drawn live from the pattern texture `assets/gallifrey/textures/gui/title/trustable.png` (a 2048×2048 tile that repeats without seams).
- **Miracle (the time vortex):** drawn live from `assets/gallifrey/textures/gui/title/vortex_red.png` and `vortex_cyan.png`.
